# Evaluation methodology

Filled progressively. Every number here is regenerated by `make eval`.

## What we claim
- Relative lift of the ML policy over Razorpay's default T+1/T+2/T+3 retry schedule on synthetic data calibrated to the public 55-65% raw-recovery band.
- Correct abstention under a `null_uplift` world.
- Off-policy estimates (IPS, SNIPS, DR) that track randomized A/B ground truth, with reported estimator error.

## What we do not claim
- Absolute rupee figures for any real merchant.
- Generalization to failure taxonomies Razorpay test mode cannot emit.
